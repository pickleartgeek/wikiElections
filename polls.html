ProbCalc — win probability

Each candidate's BaseCalc share is converted into a Gamma(α, β=1) shape parameter (α scales with total effective polling weight). Every simulated election draws one Gamma(αi, 1) variate per candidate via inverse-CDF sampling, normalizes the draws to vote shares, and records the winner. Win probability = share of simulations won.
